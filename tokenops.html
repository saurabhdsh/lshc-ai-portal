Token

Every token metered, routed and accounted for.

FinOps rebuilt for generative AI. TokenOps meters every token across models, teams and use cases, routes each request to the right-sized model, and keeps AI spend predictable as adoption scales.

Capabilities

What TokenOps does
4.1

Metering and chargeback

Token use and cost attributed to each team, product, study or use case.

4.2

Smart routing

Each request goes to the smallest model that meets its quality bar.

4.3

Budget guardrails

Alerts and hard limits per team, before the invoice surprises anyone.

4.4

Caching and prompt tuning

Repeated answers are reused and oversized prompts are trimmed.

4.5

Model portfolio view

Cost, quality and latency per model side by side, with quality fed from QEVAL.

4.6

Forecasting

Projected spend as adoption grows, for finance and programme planning.

How it works

Meter, attribute, optimise, govern
1Meter. One gateway records every request across model providers, tools and teams.
2Attribute. Usage is tagged to team, product, study and use case, ready for chargeback.
3Optimise. Routing, caching and prompt tuning lower cost while quality stays above the bar.
4Govern. Budgets, alerts and forecasts keep finance, IT and the business on one number.
